About the University

Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM) is the first overseas campus established by a Chinese national key university and the first Chinese university campus outside of China. It is ranked #207 in the world by QS World University Rankings, making it one of the most prestigious universities in Asia.

The university offers a world-class education environment combining rigorous academic standards, cutting-edge research facilities, and a multicultural campus community — preparing graduates for global careers in technology and engineering.
