PROJECT 04 / DESKTOP — TUNISIA

DATLY — 1st AI Software Suite for Oudoul Ichhed in Tunisia (عدول إشهاد)

The first dedicated software suite for Oudoul Ichhed (عدول إشهاد / Notaires) in Tunisia. Engineered from scratch to automate notary office workflows, reducing legal contract assembly time from 45 minutes down to 12–15 seconds while ensuring zero mathematical or typographical errors in official deeds. Launched in September 2026 and already deployed & sold to commercial notary offices across Tunisia.

Key features & AI modules
  • 1st Oudoul Ichhed Software in Tunisia: Pioneered digital transformation for Tunisian notary practices (عدل إشهاد), launched Sep 2026 with immediate commercial sales.
  • Biometric Client Check-In (< 100ms): OpenCV YuNet & SFace ONNX neural networks run 100% offline on local hardware to recognize arriving clients instantly at reception.
  • Multimodal AI Document Scanner & OCR: Extracts Tunisian National IDs (CIN Recto-Verso), Property Titles (Titre Foncier / شهادة ملكية), and Death Certificates (حجة وفاة) via Gemini Vision & GPT-4o LLMs.
  • AI Voice Dictation & Clause Parsing: Converts spoken notary dictations into structured legal preamble variables and contract clauses (الفصول) automatically.
  • "Hasibat Al-Farida" Inheritance Engine: Resolves multi-generational successions (المناسخات المتعاقبة), fractional shares, and mandatory bequests (الوصية الواجبة - Art. 191 CPS) into formatted legal deeds.
  • Enterprise Desktop Security & Sync: Local TCP Socket Server for multi-station office sync, DPAPI encrypted API keys, and PyInstaller / Inno Setup Windows installer bundle.
